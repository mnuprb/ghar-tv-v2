Top 10 Most Expensive Localities in Mumbai

Mumbai's most coveted ultra-luxury micro-markets — ranked by verified IGR-registered PSF, street-record transactions, brand prestige and UHNWI demand. Mumbai's ultra-luxury residential market is a category of one in India. Its scarcity-driven coastal corridor — Worli, Malabar Hill, Altamount, and Carmichael Road — concentrates more verified ₹100 crore-plus deals per square kilometre than any other Indian city. The 2024 IGR data shows 683 registered transactions in Worli alone, totalling ₹7,326 crore, while a single Naman Xana apartment set a ₹2.83 lakh psf benchmark that rewrote the South Mumbai ceiling.

Five distinct corridors define the rankings: the Worli–Sea Face spine of branded residences and UHNWI buyers; the Malabar Hill-to-Breach Candy belt of Arabian Sea promontory bungalows and old-money heritage homes; the South Mumbai street-record corridor along Carmichael and Altamount Roads; the Bandra-West Bollywood cluster anchored at Pali Hill and Bandstand; and the emerging Worli–Dadar West corridor of new corporate-buyer supply.

At ₹2.83 lakh psf, Worli's Naman Xana now anchors India's single most expensive residential street — with 683 IGR-registered deals in 2024 worth ₹7,326 crore, more than the next three South Mumbai micro-markets combined.

How we ranked these localities

Each micro-market is evaluated against verified Maharashtra IGR transactions, then cross-referenced with Knight Frank, Anarock, and JLL benchmarks. Rankings weight verified record PSF (single highest deal), the average PSF band across the 2024–25 window, notable transaction volume above ₹100 crore, and recency. Off-market and unverified broker listings are excluded. The final ordering reflects evidence through Q1 2026.
