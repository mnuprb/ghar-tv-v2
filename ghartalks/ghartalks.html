About the Show

India's Real Estate
Talk Show

GharTalks is Ghar.tv's signature video talk show — the flagship media property of the platform. Think "How I Built This" meets Indian real estate. Long-form conversations with the people who shape how India lives: developer CEOs, market experts, PropTech founders, brokers, architects, and investors.

Every episode opens doors. A single recording produces a GharTalks video episode and a written editorial piece for /voices — two surfaces from one source. GharTalks is the headline show; the /voices section is the network.
